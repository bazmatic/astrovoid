## Rulings

- Burst timing uses seconds, while Encounter dt remains frame units; an aligned released trigger finishes its
  queue, and a released unaligned trigger cancels it, matching the existing hunter behavior.
- `on_target` and `gun_enabled` controls are introduced with the first burst user (Task 2), ahead of the remaining
  extended pilot controls in Task 3. This makes the burst usable without a pilot.
- Gun requirements are checked on the constructed trait instance, so burst cadence requires `burstFire` without
  imposing that requirement on existing reload guns.
- The fire cooldown calculation is shared through `cooldown_for_upgrade(level)`; player ship callers retain
  `calculate_fire_cooldown(ship)` and the same formula and thresholds.
